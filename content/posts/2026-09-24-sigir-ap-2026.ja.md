情報検索に関する国際会議であるThe 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (ACM SIGIR-AP 2026)に論文が2本採択されました。

  - Sohei Taniguchi, Taiga Sasaki, Takehiro Yamamoto, Hiroaki Ohshima and Sumio Fujita: Mitigating Catastrophic Forgetting in Domain-Specific Ad-hoc Retrieval via Model Merging, Proceedings of the 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (ACM SIGIR-AP 2026), to appear, December 2026.
  - Taiyo Ikemoto and Takehiro Yamamoto: Analyzing the Effects of Query Type and Ranking Methods on the Stance Distribution of Search Results on Controversial Topics, Proceedings of the 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (ACM SIGIR-AP 2026), to appear, December 2026.

12月にシンガポールで発表予定です。
