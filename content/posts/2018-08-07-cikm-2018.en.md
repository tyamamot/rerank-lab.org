Our paper "Exploring People's Attitudes and Behaviors toward Careful Information Seeking in Web Search" by me, Prof. Yusuke Yamamoto at Shizuoka University, and Sumio Fujita at Yahoo Japan Corporation, accepted as a full paper at CIKM2018.

This paper investigates how people carefully search for the Web to obtain credible and accurate information. The goal of this study is to better understand people's attitudes toward careful information seeking via Web search, and the relationship between such attitudes and their daily search behaviors.

Takehiro Yamamoto, Yusuke Yamamoto and Sumio Fujita:  
Exploring People’s Attitudes and Behaviors Toward Careful Information Seeking in Web Search  
Proceedings of the 27th ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM 2018), October 2018 (to appear).
